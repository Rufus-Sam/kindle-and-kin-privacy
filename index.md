# Kindle and Kin Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 26, 2026

Kindle and Kin ("the app") is made by K&K Digital ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: **it doesn't collect any.**

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app:

- has **no account** or sign-up
- makes **no internet connections**
- contains **no analytics, advertising or tracking** tools
- does **not** sell or share any data, because it never has any

## What stays on your device

Anything you add or change is saved **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- quotes you save to Favorites
- occasions you add (names, dates, kind)
- greeting-card text you save
- your reminder settings

We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it. It may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud or Google), which Apple and Google handle under their own privacy policies.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to send notifications. They are **local notifications**: your device schedules and shows them. No server is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device settings at any time.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, the app opens your device's standard share sheet with the quote or card you picked. The content goes only to the app or person **you** choose, and only when you tap Share. After that, that app's privacy policy applies.

## Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
